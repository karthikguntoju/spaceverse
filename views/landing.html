Spaceverse started as a complaint: space is the most astonishing thing there is, and most of us only ever meet it as a flat diagram in a textbook. So we rebuilt it — to scale, in a browser tab, with real orbital mechanics, real mission data and a shuttle you can ride out of the atmosphere.

No install, no plugin, no astronomy degree. Open a tab and you are in orbit.

Field Notes

A short briefing on where you are going

Numbers worth carrying with you. Every one of them is modelled somewhere inside Spaceverse.

One Astronomical Unit 149.6M km

The average Earth–Sun distance, and the ruler astronomers use for everything inside the solar system.

Sunlight Delay 8 min 20 s

How long a photon takes to reach you. The Sun you see has already moved on without you.

Solar System Age 4.6 bn yrs

Condensed out of a collapsing molecular cloud. Earth showed up in the first hundred million years of it.

Orbital Velocity 7.8 km/s

What low Earth orbit costs you. Not height — speed. Orbiting is falling and missing the ground.

Jupiter's Mass 318 ×

Earths. More than twice every other planet combined, and it shields the inner system from stray comets.

Tracked Debris 36,000+

Objects larger than 10 cm circling Earth right now. The traffic simulator lets you make it worse.

Observable Galaxies 2 trillion

Estimated across the observable universe. The deep-field ride flies you through a slice of them.

Universe Age 13.8 bn yrs

Measured from the cosmic microwave background — the oldest light anything has ever recorded.
