Headset setup

SpaceVerse on Meta Quest

Fly through the solar system, ride in a spaceship cockpit, or play the Arcade on a virtual screen. Open SpaceVerse in the headset’s browser.

Get into VR

  1. Open Browser on your Quest and visit a SpaceVerse address that starts with https://. Sign in when prompted.
  2. Pick an experience below. Select Enter headset VR for the solar system, ride or Asteroid Dodge. On the other games, select Play in headset VR.
  3. Point a Touch controller and pull the trigger to choose an option. Where hand tracking is available, aim and pinch to select. Use Touch controllers for actions that need a thumbstick or button.

Immersive flight

VR Solar System

Fly freely or pick Scan Hunt for a timed challenge. Use the left stick to move, the right stick to turn or change height, and the trigger to scan planets and moons. Squeeze the side grip to bring up controls for auto-flight, low orbit, and the cockpit.

Immersive cockpit

Deep Space Ride

The ship flies the route while you look around. Squeeze the side grip to pause and open ride controls. Resume, skip a section, restart, or turn on Comfort mode to reduce rolls. You can turn off jumpscares before entering. The trigger fires the turret during a hazard and can advance the ride when no target is hit.

Open Deep Space Ride →

Immersive arcade

Asteroid Dodge

Sit facing forward and select Asteroid Dodge in the headset launcher. Pull the trigger to start the countdown, then gently turn your head left or right to avoid rocks. Squeeze the side grip for pause, resume, or restart.

Open Asteroid Dodge →

Virtual screen

Arcade games

Choose one of the eleven screen games. On the virtual screen, use the trigger to click or drag, the left stick to steer where supported, and A for the game’s main keyboard action. The screen shows extra buttons for games that use them. Use the side grip to pause and try Recenter screen if the panel is off to one side.

Browse the Arcade →

If headset mode is unavailable

Update the headset and its browser, then open SpaceVerse directly in Browser. Headset mode requires immersive WebXR support and an HTTPS address trusted by your Quest. A laptop’s localhost address only opens the site on the laptop.

Use the in-game exit or the Meta button to return to the browser. For phone viewers, the VR pages still offer Gyro look and Phone VR (Cardboard).
