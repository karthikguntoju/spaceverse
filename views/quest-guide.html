SpaceVerse on Meta Quest 3

Play seven Meta Quest 3 exclusive arcade VR games built natively around dual 6DoF Touch Plus controllers, explore the solar system, ride in a 3D cockpit, or play classic Arcade titles on a virtual screen.

Get into Meta Quest 3 VR

  1. Open Meta Quest Browser inside your Meta Quest 3 and visit a SpaceVerse address that starts with https://. Sign in when prompted.
  2. Pick a game or experience below. All seven VR Arcade games are exclusive to Meta Quest 3 and require both Touch Plus controllers.
  3. Use A on your right controller to start/pause, B to recenter, and Grip (or Y in Orbital Intercept) for the VR menu.

Meta Quest 3 Exclusive · Dual Wield

Orbital Intercept

Defend Low Earth Orbit against waves of rogue drones, magma meteors, and the Orbital Siege Core. Hold Left Trigger to deploy your Hex Aegis Shield and deflect bolts back at enemies, squeeze Left Grip at 100% for EMP, aim with Right Trigger to fire your Twin Plasma Blaster, and hold Right Grip to charge your piercing Rail-Lance. Press Y for menu.

VR Solar System

Fly freely or pick Scan Hunt for a timed challenge. Use the left stick to move, the right stick to turn or change height, and the trigger to scan planets and moons. Squeeze the side grip to bring up controls for auto-flight, low orbit, and the cockpit.

Immersive cockpit

Deep Space Ride

The ship flies the route while you look around. Squeeze the side grip to pause and open ride controls. Resume, skip a section, restart, or turn on Comfort mode to reduce rolls. You can turn off jumpscares before entering. The trigger fires the turret during a hazard and can advance the ride when no target is hit.

If headset mode is unavailable

Update the headset and its browser, then open SpaceVerse directly in Browser. Headset mode requires immersive WebXR support and an HTTPS address trusted by your Quest. A laptop’s localhost address only opens the site on the laptop.

Use the in-game exit or the Meta button to return to the browser. For phone viewers, the VR pages still offer Gyro look and Phone VR (Cardboard).
